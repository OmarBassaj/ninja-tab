What it does

Everything works in your browser, with no account and nothing to set up beyond your rules.

Disguise any site

Pick one of ten ready-made looks or use any title and icon you like. A rule can cover a whole site, only its subdomains, or one section like reddit.com/r/games.

Boss key

Press Alt+Shift+B and the tab turns into Google search. It can also mute the tab and blur the page. Press again to come back.

Panic button

Hide every matching tab at once, or send the current tab to another site and remove it from history.

History cleanup

Visits to your disguised sites are deleted when the tab closes, so they stay out of address-bar suggestions and new-tab shortcuts.

PIN lock

Keep your rules behind a 4-digit PIN, so nobody else can open the settings and see what you hide.

Background mode

See the real page while you use it. The disguise appears only when you switch to another tab.

Install in a minute

Ninja Tab isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so Chrome asks you to add it by hand. It takes three steps.

The fastest way: add Ninja Tab from the Chrome Web Store with one click.

Add to Chrome

  1. Download and unzip

    Unzip the file. You'll get a folder called ninja-tab. Keep it somewhere it won't be deleted, because Chrome loads the extension from it.

    Download .zip

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Copy this address into a new tab, then switch on Developer mode in the top right corner.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the ninja-tab folder. Then pin the ninja from the puzzle icon so it's one click away.

Chrome may show a note about developer-mode extensions when it starts. That's normal for extensions added this way.

Nothing leaves your browser

Ninja Tab has no account, no servers and no tracking. Your rules are saved by Chrome on your device, and if you use Chrome sync they follow you to your other computers. Preset icons load from the sites they belong to, the same way any web page loads its icon.

Questions

Why isn't it a one-click install?

Chrome only allows one-click installs from the Chrome Web Store. Once Ninja Tab is published there, the button at the top of this page will install it directly.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Opera?

Yes. They're built on the same engine as Chrome. Use edge://extensions in Edge, brave://extensions in Brave and opera://extensions in Opera for step 2.

Can someone still tell what I'm looking at?

Ninja Tab changes what the tab bar and your history show. The address bar still shows the real address, and anyone looking at your screen sees the real page unless you press the Boss Key.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the new version and replace the old ninja-tab folder with it. Then open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow on Ninja Tab. Your rules and settings stay.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There are no ads and no paid version.
